# 01. Vision

## The product

**NextGeneration Class** is a private app for **one primary school teacher** in Tunisia (1ère to 6ème année) who teaches her own after-school groups and private lessons. She decides which subjects she teaches and creates them in the app. Her pupils are young (6 to 12): parents are the main readers, and a child gets their own login only when the teacher wants it. Her students are already enrolled and have already paid. The app makes her daily work more organized, faster and more professional, and keeps students and parents informed.

It is **not** a website where students watch courses. It is **a digital assistant for a private teaching activity**.

## The problem

A private teacher with several groups and individual students spends a lot of time on work that is not teaching:
- remembering who comes when, and which group is online or in person
- finding the right series or document again, or rewriting it
- tracking who submitted homework and what still needs correction
- noticing early that a student is falling behind or missing sessions
- answering parents who want to know how their child is doing

The app removes this load so the teacher can focus on teaching.

## The success test

When the teacher opens the app, she knows within seconds:

1. What session is next
2. Who is attending
3. What needs correction
4. Who has not submitted their work
5. What she should prepare
6. What she needs to send to students
7. Which students need attention

If the app answers these seven questions quickly, it is succeeding.

## Product principles

1. **Teacher comfort before feature count.** A missing feature is better than a confusing one.
2. **Common actions in one tap.** Attendance, assigning work, opening the next correction.
3. **Reuse before rewrite.** Upload existing documents, duplicate old quizzes and series.
4. **Few screens.** About 15 for the teacher; modals and side panels for creation.
5. **Simple for students and parents.** A parent understands their child's situation in less than a minute.
6. **Free first.** Open-source tools and free tiers; any paid service is approved by the client first.
7. **Works without AI.** AI is an optional helper added later, never a dependency.
8. **Supports both online and in-person teaching.** Meet links for online sessions, printable PDFs for paper work.

## Measurable goals

| Goal | Target |
|---|---|
| Mark attendance for a group of 8 | under 15 seconds |
| Assign an existing document to a group | under 1 minute |
| Go from the home screen to the next correction | 1 click |
| Create a 10-question QCM | under 10 minutes |
| A parent understands their child's situation | under 1 minute |
| Home screen load on 4G | under 2 seconds |
| Parallel tools the teacher still needs (paper attendance, WhatsApp for homework) | none after Release 2 |

## Final goal, in the client's words

> "N7el l'application, nal9a chnowa 3andi lyoum, chnowa lezemni ncorrecti, chnowa lezemni n7adher, w kol chay mratab."

*(I open the app, I find what I have today, what I need to correct, what I need to prepare, and everything is in order.)*
